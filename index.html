Common Questions

Yes your initial consultation at Optima is complimentary. It's a conversation, not a commitment. We will take the time to understand your goals, explain your options, and answer every question. Nothing is assumed, nothing is rushed.

We accept most major insurance providers including Daman, AXA Gulf, NextCare, MetLife, Cigna, Oman Insurance, NAS, and Al Ain Ahlia. If your provider is not listed, we welcome direct-pay patients and are happy to discuss flexible payment options.

It depends on the scope of your case. A simple veneer treatment typically takes 2–3 visits over 2–3 weeks. A full smile makeover involving multiple treatments is planned on a case-by-case basis. We use digital smile design so you can preview your result before any treatment begins.

All facial aesthetic treatments at Optima are performed by licensed practitioners in a clinical setting. We use only regulated, branded products. Our approach is always conservative natural-looking results, never overdone. We will discuss your goals thoroughly before recommending any treatment.

Our wellness offering includes body contouring, slimming treatments, weight management consultations, IV drip therapy, and metabolism support programmes. Each programme is personalised and starts with a thorough assessment. We treat the whole person, not just the presenting concern.

Optima Health Care is located in Jumeirah, Dubai. We are open Saturday through Thursday, 9:00am to 9:00pm. You can book a consultation online, via WhatsApp, or by calling us directly. Our team typically confirms appointments within 2 hours.
