Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

What is body contouring in Dubai? +

Body contouring refers to non-surgical treatments that reduce localised fat deposits and reshape the body. At Optima Health Care, our body contouring services include cryolipolysis (fat freezing) and EMS body sculpting. These treatments target stubborn fat that doesn't respond to diet and exercise, with no surgery, no anaesthesia, and no downtime.

What are the benefits of IV drip therapy? +

IV drip therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and hydration directly into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system for maximum absorption. Benefits include enhanced energy, improved immune function, faster recovery, skin radiance, and rehydration. At Optima Health Care, all IV drip formulations are prescribed and administered by DHA-licensed doctors.

Is cryolipolysis (fat freezing) safe? +

Yes. Cryolipolysis is an FDA-cleared, clinically proven treatment that targets and destroys fat cells through controlled cooling. It is non-invasive, requires no anaesthesia, and has an excellent safety profile. Results appear gradually over 6–12 weeks as the body naturally eliminates the treated fat cells.

How many IV drip sessions do I need? +

This depends entirely on your health goals. Some patients benefit from a single session - such as before or after travel, or during illness recovery. Others follow a regular wellness programme of weekly or monthly drips. Our doctors assess your needs at consultation and recommend a frequency that suits your goals and lifestyle.

Can I combine slimming and wellness treatments? +

Absolutely - and we often recommend it. For example, combining cryolipolysis with EMS sculpting accelerates body reshaping by simultaneously reducing fat and building muscle. Adding IV drip therapy optimises your energy and recovery throughout the programme. Our doctors design combined protocols based on your specific goals and starting point.
