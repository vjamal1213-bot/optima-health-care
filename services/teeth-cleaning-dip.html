What is professional teeth cleaning?

More than a brush — a thorough reset for your oral health.

Professional teeth cleaning (also called scaling and polishing) removes tartar, plaque and stains that regular brushing simply cannot reach. Over time, plaque hardens into tartar — particularly along the gum line and between teeth — and can only be removed by a dental professional using specialist tools.

At our DIP clinic in Junction Mall, Dubai Investment Park, our DHA-licensed dentists use ultrasonic scalers and hand instruments to thoroughly clean every surface of your teeth, above and below the gum line. We serve patients from across DIP, Discovery Gardens, Green Community, Al Furjan, Jebel Ali Village and beyond.

What's included

Everything in your teeth cleaning appointment at our DIP clinic.

Step 1

Oral health assessment

A visual examination of your teeth, gums and mouth. We check for cavities, gum disease, early signs of oral cancer and any concerns before cleaning begins.

Step 2

Supragingival scaling

Removal of tartar and plaque from all visible tooth surfaces above the gum line using an ultrasonic scaler. Fast, effective and comfortable for most patients.

Step 3

Subgingival scaling

Where needed, we clean below the gum line to remove tartar deposits that cause gum inflammation and periodontitis — the leading cause of tooth loss in adults.

Step 4

Polish & stain removal

A professional polishing paste smooths tooth surfaces and removes stains from coffee, tea, and food. Leaves your teeth visibly cleaner and feeling fresh.

Step 5

Fluoride treatment

An optional fluoride application to strengthen enamel and protect against cavities — particularly beneficial for patients with a history of decay or sensitive teeth.

Step 6

Oral hygiene advice

Your dentist will give you personalised guidance on brushing technique, interdental cleaning and the right products for your teeth — so results last longer between visits.

Why it matters

Why professional teeth cleaning every 6 months protects your smile long-term.

No matter how well you brush and floss at home, plaque accumulates in areas that are difficult to reach — especially the spaces between teeth and along the gum line. Left untreated, plaque hardens into tartar within 24 to 72 hours and becomes impossible to remove without professional instruments.

Tartar build-up is the primary cause of gum disease (gingivitis and periodontitis), which is linked not only to tooth loss but to systemic health conditions including heart disease and diabetes. Regular professional cleaning at our DIP clinic intercepts these problems before they become serious.

For most patients, a cleaning every 6 months is sufficient. Those with active gum disease or a higher cavity risk may benefit from more frequent visits — your DHA-licensed dentist at our Dubai Investment Park clinic will advise the schedule that's right for you.
