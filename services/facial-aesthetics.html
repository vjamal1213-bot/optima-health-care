Treatments

A curated menu of medical-grade treatments.

Botox & Anti-Wrinkle

Botulinum toxin injections that relax targeted muscles to smooth forehead lines, frown lines, crow's feet, and more. Results develop over 3–5 days and last 3–4 months.

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid fillers that restore lost volume, define facial contours, and plump lips or cheeks. Results are immediate and last 9–18 months. Fully reversible.

HydraFacial

A medical-grade resurfacing treatment that cleanses, exfoliates, extracts, and hydrates in one session. Zero downtime. Immediate luminosity. Suitable for all skin types, year-round.

PRP Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma derived from your own blood, reinjected to stimulate collagen, improve skin texture, and accelerate rejuvenation. 100% natural, zero foreign substances.

Skin Boosters

Micro-injections of hyaluronic acid and vitamins that deeply hydrate and improve skin quality from within. Ideal for dehydrated, dull, or crepey skin - especially in Dubai's dry climate.

Thread Lift

Dissolvable PDO threads that provide an immediate lifting effect and stimulate collagen over time. A non-surgical alternative to a facelift - with minimal downtime and natural-looking results.

Common Questions

Answers to what patients ask most.

Is Botox safe in Dubai? +

Yes, when administered by a DHA-licensed medical professional. At Optima Health Care, all facial aesthetic treatments including Botox are performed by licensed doctors trained in advanced injection techniques. The Dubai Health Authority regulates all aesthetic medicine practices, and our clinic licence DHA-F-9476361 is fully current.

How long do dermal fillers last? +

Dermal fillers typically last 9 to 18 months depending on the product, treatment area, and individual metabolism. Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved at any time if you wish to reverse the effect. We use only premium FDA-approved filler brands for safety and longevity.

What is the difference between Botox and fillers? +

Botox relaxes targeted facial muscles to reduce dynamic wrinkles caused by expressions - such as frown lines and crow's feet. Dermal fillers restore volume and enhance contours - such as lips, cheeks, and jawline. Many patients benefit from a tailored combination of both for a complete facial refresh.

Is there downtime after facial aesthetic treatments? +

Most of our facial aesthetic treatments require minimal to no downtime. Botox and fillers may cause mild bruising or swelling for 24–48 hours. HydraFacial has zero downtime - your skin looks luminous immediately. PRP and thread lift may involve 2–5 days of minor swelling. We discuss this fully at your consultation.

What makes Optima different from other aesthetic clinics in Dubai? +

All treatments at Optima are performed by DHA-licensed doctors - not nurses or technicians. We use only FDA-approved products, take a conservative-first approach, and never apply a one-size-fits-all treatment plan. Your consultation is comprehensive and unhurried, and we are transparent about what will and will not work for you.
