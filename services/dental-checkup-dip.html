Why dental checkups matter

Most dental problems are painless — until they aren't.

Cavities, gum disease, cracked teeth and even oral cancer rarely cause pain in their early stages. By the time discomfort appears, the problem is often significantly advanced — and significantly more expensive to treat. A routine dental checkup every 6 months at our DIP clinic is the single most effective way to catch these issues before they escalate.

Our DHA-licensed dentists at Junction Mall, Dubai Investment Park perform a thorough visual examination, assess your gums and bite, and use digital X-rays where needed to see what the naked eye cannot. Your checkup, step by step

What happens at your dental checkup at our DIP clinic.

Step 1

Dental & medical history

For new patients, we begin by reviewing your dental and medical history — medications, allergies, any history of gum disease, pain or sensitivity. This informs your entire care plan.

Step 2

Tooth-by-tooth examination

Your dentist examines every tooth surface for cavities, cracks, chips, worn enamel, and existing fillings or restorations that may need attention. Findings are charted for your records.

Step 3

Gum health assessment

We measure the depth of the pockets around each tooth to assess gum health. Early gingivitis or periodontitis can be reversed when caught here. Swelling, recession and bleeding are noted.

Step 4

Digital X-rays (when needed)

Digital dental X-rays reveal cavities between teeth, bone levels, the roots of teeth, and impacted wisdom teeth — all invisible to the naked eye. Low-radiation, instant results.

Step 5

Bite & jaw assessment

We check how your upper and lower teeth fit together and assess the jaw joints (TMJ). Teeth grinding, clenching and bite misalignment can cause significant long-term damage if undetected.

Step 6

Treatment plan & prevention

Your dentist walks you through all findings and, where treatment is needed, presents clear options with pricing before anything is booked. Prevention advice is tailored to your specific oral health profile.

The case for early detection

Catching it early costs a fraction of treating it late.

A small cavity detected at a routine checkup can be treated with a simple filling (AED 150–250). Left undetected for another year, that same cavity may reach the nerve — requiring root canal treatment (AED 800–1,500) and a dental crown (AED 1,200–2,500). The cost difference is enormous. The discomfort difference is even greater.

The same principle applies to gum disease. Gingivitis caught at a 6-monthly checkup can be fully reversed with a professional clean. Advanced periodontitis — the leading cause of tooth loss in adults — requires months of deep cleaning or surgical intervention, and the bone loss is permanent.

At our DIP clinic in Junction Mall, Dubai Investment Park, we see this difference every day. The patients who come in for regular checkups spend significantly less on dental treatment over their lifetime — and keep their natural teeth significantly longer.
