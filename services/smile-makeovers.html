What is a Smile Makeover?

More than aesthetics - a complete reimagining of your smile.

A smile makeover is a personalised combination of cosmetic dentistry procedures that addresses the shape, colour, alignment, and symmetry of your teeth. Unlike a single treatment, a makeover considers your entire facial aesthetic - how your smile looks, feels, and functions in harmony with who you are.

At Optima Health Care in Dubai Investment Park, our DHA-licensed cosmetic dentists combine advanced digital smile design with exceptional clinical craft to create results that look genuinely natural - never overdone. We serve patients from across Dubai, including Discovery Gardens, Al Furjan, Jebel Ali, Dubai South, Motor City, and Sports City.

Treatments

Every treatment you need, in one place.

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin ceramic shells bonded to the front of your teeth. The gold standard for transforming shape, colour, and length - with results that last over a decade.

Teeth Whitening

Philips Zoom in-clinic whitening delivers up to 8 shades brighter in a single session. Professional-grade results with minimal sensitivity.

Invisalign

Clear aligners that straighten teeth discreetly - no brackets, no wires. Virtually invisible and removable, with no disruption to your daily life.

Dental Implants

Titanium implants that replace missing teeth with a permanent, natural-looking result. The only restorative solution that preserves bone and feels like your own tooth.

Composite Bonding

Tooth-coloured resin sculpted directly onto your teeth to correct chips, gaps, and minor imperfections. Fast, affordable, and completely reversible.

Dental Crowns

Full-coverage ceramic restorations that protect and restore damaged or heavily filled teeth - indistinguishable from your natural teeth in colour and translucency.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

What is a smile makeover in Dubai? +

A smile makeover is a personalised combination of cosmetic dentistry treatments - such as porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, Invisalign, and composite bonding - designed to improve the appearance of your smile. At Optima Health Care, our DHA-licensed cosmetic dentists tailor every plan to your facial aesthetics, tooth structure, and personal goals.

How long does a smile makeover take? +

The timeline depends on the treatments involved. A simple whitening and bonding makeover can be completed in one or two appointments. Veneers typically take two to three visits over two to four weeks. If Invisalign is part of your plan, treatment usually runs six to eighteen months. We provide a clear timeline at your complimentary consultation.

Are smile makeovers painful? +

Modern cosmetic dentistry is highly comfortable. Procedures such as teeth whitening and Invisalign are non-invasive and virtually pain-free. Veneers and implants are performed under local anaesthetic so you feel no discomfort during treatment. Some mild sensitivity may occur afterwards but typically resolves within a few days.

Is Optima Health Care DHA licensed for cosmetic dentistry? +

Yes. All our dentists hold active DHA (Dubai Health Authority) licences. Our clinic licence number is DHA-F-9476361. You can verify this on the DHA Sheryan portal. We maintain strict clinical standards across all dental and aesthetic treatments.

Can I see what my smile will look like before treatment? +

Yes. We use Digital Smile Design to create a preview of your proposed smile using photographs and digital simulation. You'll see a realistic visualisation of your final result before any treatment begins, and we refine the design until you're completely happy with the direction.
