Invisalign in Dubai:
Complete Guide — Cost, Process & Results (2026)

By Dr. Anas Mathari · 3 July 2026 · 5 min read

Invisalign has transformed orthodontics for adults and teenagers who want straighter teeth without the look — and inconvenience — of traditional metal braces. In Dubai, Invisalign is now one of the most popular cosmetic dental treatments, with thousands of patients completing treatment each year.

If you're considering Invisalign at a Dubai clinic, this guide covers how the system works, who it's suitable for, how long treatment takes, and what you can expect to pay in AED.

How Does Invisalign Work?

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, removable clear plastic aligners to gradually shift your teeth into their correct positions. Each aligner is worn for approximately one to two weeks before moving to the next in the series, with each set making small, precise adjustments to tooth position.

Unlike braces, Invisalign aligners are:

  • Virtually invisible — most people won't notice you're wearing them
  • Removable — take them out to eat, drink, and brush your teeth
  • Comfortable — no metal wires or brackets to irritate your gums and cheeks
  • Predictable — digital treatment planning lets you see your projected outcome before you start

For treatment to be effective, aligners must be worn for 20–22 hours per day. The discipline required is the main reason Invisalign suits motivated adults and older teenagers particularly well.

For mild to moderate alignment issues, Invisalign typically delivers equivalent results to braces — with significantly more comfort and discretion. For very complex orthodontic cases involving severe bite problems or significant tooth rotation, traditional braces may still be the more effective option. Your dentist will advise you which is appropriate for your specific situation.

Am I a Candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign is suitable for correcting a wide range of issues, including:

  • Crowded teeth
  • Gaps between teeth (spacing)
  • Overbite, underbite, and crossbite
  • Open bite
  • Generally misaligned teeth

Invisalign works for most adults and teenagers, including patients who have previously had braces and experienced some relapse. It is not recommended for young children whose teeth are still developing, or for cases requiring significant vertical tooth movement or complex jaw correction.

The Invisalign Process at Our Dubai Clinic

Step 1 — Digital Scan & Assessment

We use the iTero Element intraoral scanner to take a precise 3D digital scan of your teeth — no messy impressions. The Invisalign ClinCheck software then generates a step-by-step digital treatment plan showing how your teeth will move and what your final smile will look like.

Step 2 — Custom Aligners Fabricated

Your personal set of aligners is manufactured by Align Technology in the USA using precision-cut SmartTrack material. They typically arrive at our clinic within 2–3 weeks.

Step 3 — Wearing Your Aligners

You'll receive your aligners and wear each set for 1–2 weeks, moving through the series as directed. We schedule check-in appointments every 6–8 weeks to monitor progress and provide the next sets of aligners.

Step 4 — Retainers

Once treatment is complete, you'll wear retainers (usually at night) to hold your teeth in their new positions. This is a crucial and permanent part of orthodontic treatment — without retainers, teeth have a natural tendency to shift back.

Choosing an Invisalign Provider in Dubai

Invisalign is only available through certified dental providers. When choosing a provider in Dubai, consider:

  • Invisalign certification and tier — providers are rated as Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Diamond based on case volume and outcomes. Higher-tier providers have treated significantly more cases.
  • iTero scanning — digital scanning produces more accurate aligners than traditional impressions.
  • DHA licence — all dentists practising in Dubai must hold a valid DHA (Dubai Health Authority) licence.
  • Clear treatment plan — a reputable clinic will show you your ClinCheck simulation before you commit.
  • Refinement policy — confirm whether refinements (additional aligner sets to fine-tune the result) are included in the quoted price.
