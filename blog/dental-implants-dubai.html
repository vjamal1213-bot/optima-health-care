Dental Implants in Dubai:
Costs, Procedure & What to Expect (2026)

By Dr. Anas Mathari · 3 July 2026 · 6 min read

Dental implants are widely regarded as the gold standard for replacing missing teeth — and for good reason. Unlike removable dentures or bridges, implants function exactly like natural teeth, with a titanium root that fuses permanently to your jawbone.

If you're considering dental implants at a clinic in Dubai, this guide covers everything you need to know: how the procedure works, what it costs in AED, how long it takes, and what to look for in an implant dentist.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant consists of three parts:

  1. The implant post — a titanium screw inserted into your jawbone that acts as the tooth root
  2. The abutment — a connector piece that attaches above the gum line
  3. The crown — the custom-made, visible tooth placed on top

Because the titanium post actually fuses with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration, implants feel and function like natural teeth. You can eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults who have lost one or more teeth are suitable candidates, provided:

  • You have sufficient bone density in your jaw to support the implant
  • Your gums are healthy (active gum disease must be treated first)
  • You don't smoke heavily — smoking significantly reduces implant success rates
  • Any systemic conditions such as diabetes are well-controlled

If bone density is a concern, bone grafting can often be performed beforehand to prepare the site — a common procedure at specialist dental clinics in Dubai.

The Dental Implant Procedure: Stage by Stage

Getting dental implants in Dubai typically involves three stages spread over several months:

Stage 1 — Consultation & Assessment

Your dentist will take digital X-rays or a 3D CBCT scan to assess your bone structure and gum health, and create a personalised treatment plan. At our Dubai Investment Park clinic, we use digital planning technology so you can visualise your expected results before any work begins.

Stage 2 — Implant Placement

Under local anaesthesia, the titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. This is a minor procedure that typically takes 60–90 minutes per implant. Most patients experience minimal discomfort afterwards, managed comfortably with over-the-counter pain relief.

Stage 3 — Crown Placement (3–6 months later)

Once the implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, the custom-made crown is attached. At this point, your new tooth looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural one. Total treatment from first appointment to final crown typically takes 4–8 months.

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost in Dubai?

Dental implant costs in Dubai vary depending on the implant brand used, the complexity of your case, and whether additional procedures (such as bone grafting) are required. As a general guide:

TreatmentEstimated Cost (AED)
Single tooth implant (post + crown)AED 5,000 – 9,000
Bone graft (if required)AED 1,500 – 3,500
All-on-4 (full arch)AED 20,000 – 45,000+
All-on-6 (full arch)AED 25,000 – 55,000+

These figures typically include the consultation, implant post, abutment, and crown. Always confirm what is included when comparing quotes between Dubai dental clinics.

Insurance tip: Coverage for dental implants in Dubai varies significantly by policy. Contact your provider before your consultation — some plans cover a portion of implant costs when tooth loss is due to accident or injury.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care and regular dental check-ups, the implant post itself is designed to last a lifetime. The crown on top may eventually need replacing — typically after 15–25 years — depending on wear and care. This makes implants considerably more cost-effective than alternatives such as bridges, which typically need replacement every 10–15 years.

One often overlooked advantage of implants is that they prevent jawbone deterioration. When a tooth is lost, the jawbone beneath it begins to shrink over time — a process that bridges and dentures cannot stop. Implants, by stimulating the bone just like a natural root, preserve both your bone structure and your facial appearance.

Choosing an Implant Dentist in Dubai — What to Look For

Dental implant placement is a surgical procedure — the experience and qualifications of your dentist matter significantly. When choosing a dental clinic in Dubai for implants, look for:

  • DHA-licensed dentists with documented implantology training
  • 3D CBCT scanning for precise implant planning
  • Use of internationally recognised implant brands (Nobel Biocare, Straumann, Osstem)
  • A written treatment plan and fully itemised quote provided before you commit
  • Clear aftercare and follow-up protocols
