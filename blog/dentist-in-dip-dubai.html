Routine dental care in DIP: checkups and teeth cleaning

The foundation of good oral health is simple: a professional teeth cleaning and dental checkup every 6 months. This combination prevents the vast majority of dental problems — and the expensive, time-consuming treatments that follow when problems go undetected.

Dental checkups at our DIP clinic

A comprehensive dental checkup in DIP at Optima Health Care includes a full visual examination of all teeth and gums, digital X-rays where needed, a gum health and periodontal assessment, bite and jaw evaluation, and a personalised treatment plan. First consultations are complimentary for new patients.

Most patients are surprised by what a thorough checkup reveals. Cavities, early gum disease and cracked teeth are rarely painful in their early stages — but they are always visible to a trained dentist with the right diagnostic tools. Catching these issues at a routine checkup in DIP means treatment is simpler, faster and significantly cheaper.

Professional teeth cleaning at our DIP clinic

Professional teeth cleaning in DIP — also called scaling and polishing — removes tartar and plaque that builds up in areas regular brushing cannot reach. Our DHA-licensed dental hygienists use ultrasonic scalers to gently clean above and below the gum line, followed by a professional polish to remove staining and leave teeth feeling visibly cleaner.

Most DIP residents notice a significant improvement in gum health and breath freshness after their first professional clean, even those who brush and floss regularly. That is because tartar — hardened plaque — can only be removed professionally, and it accumulates regardless of how carefully you brush.

Cosmetic dentistry in DIP, Dubai Investment Park

Beyond routine care, Optima Health Care offers a full range of cosmetic dental treatments for patients in DIP and the surrounding communities.

Porcelain veneers

Dental veneers are ultra-thin porcelain shells bonded to the front surface of teeth to correct discolouration, chips, gaps and mild misalignment. Our DIP clinic uses E.max porcelain veneers for exceptional durability and natural aesthetics — the same material used by leading cosmetic dentists globally.

Invisalign clear aligners

Invisalign straightens teeth using a series of custom-made, removable clear aligners — with no metal brackets or wires. Our DHA-licensed dentists at the DIP clinic are certified Invisalign providers. We offer free Invisalign assessments for patients in Dubai Investment Park.

Teeth whitening

Professional teeth whitening at our DIP clinic delivers results that no over-the-counter product can match. We offer both in-clinic whitening (results in one hour) and take-home whitening kits with custom-fitted trays. Both use professional-grade whitening agents available only through licensed dental clinics.

Dental implants

For patients with missing teeth, dental implants are the gold standard long-term solution. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone to act as an artificial root, with a custom crown placed on top. Results look, feel and function exactly like a natural tooth — and with proper care, implants can last a lifetime.

Smile makeovers

A smile makeover combines multiple cosmetic treatments — typically veneers, whitening, gum contouring and/or Invisalign — into a single coordinated treatment plan designed around your specific smile goals. Our dentists at the DIP clinic work with you to understand what you want to change, and design a treatment plan to get you there.
